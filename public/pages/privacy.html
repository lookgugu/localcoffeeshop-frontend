Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics

Most pages of this site (including the home page, the interactive state pages, the About page, the submit page and the "page not found" page) load the Google tag (Google Analytics 4). Google Analytics helps us understand things like which pages are visited and how visitors find the site. When analytics cookies are allowed, Google Analytics may set first-party cookies such as _ga and _ga_YVSXN7PM48 to tell visits apart.

Google processes this information under its own terms and privacy policy. You can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can install Google's Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Your cookie choices

The first time you visit a page with Google Analytics, a cookie banner asks for your choice. You can:

  • Accept All: allow analytics cookies and advertising cookies.
  • Reject All: deny analytics cookies and advertising cookies.
  • Customize: turn analytics cookies and advertising cookies on or off separately. Essential storage needed for the site to work is always on.

We use Google Consent Mode to pass your choice to the Google tag. When you deny a category, the Google tag is told not to use cookies for that purpose. The Google tag is still loaded on these pages either way, and Google's Consent Mode may send limited measurement information without cookies when analytics cookies are denied.

We don't currently show ads on this site. The "advertising" choice controls Google's advertising-related consent settings (ad storage, ad user data and ad personalization), which are denied unless you allow them.

Before you make a choice

Until you choose, advertising cookies are always denied. Whether analytics cookies are allowed by default depends on where your browser appears to be: we check your browser's time zone and language settings (this check happens in your browser and is not sent to us). If your time zone is in Europe (including the Azores, Canary Islands, Faroe Islands and Madeira), is UTC, or is US Pacific time, or your browser's language settings name a country in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, analytics cookies are denied until you allow them. Otherwise analytics cookies are allowed by default, and you can turn them off using the banner.

Changing your mind

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage (under the name cookie_consent), together with the date and time you made it. It is not sent to our servers. To change your choice, clear this site's data in your browser settings (sometimes called "cookies and site data"); the banner will appear again on your next visit to a page that uses it.

Information stored on your device

  • Your cookie choice, saved in local storage as described above.
  • Offline copies of the site. The home page, the interactive state pages and the "page not found" page register a service worker that stores copies of the site's pages, scripts and styles in your browser so the site loads faster and can work offline. These copies contain the site's own files, not information about you. You can remove them by clearing this site's data in your browser.
  • Google Analytics cookies, only if analytics cookies are allowed (see above).
  • A Cloudflare security cookie. Our hosting passes through Cloudflare, which may set a short-lived cookie named __cf_bm (it expires after about 30 minutes) to help tell people apart from automated bots. It is strictly necessary for security and is not used for analytics or advertising.

Your location ("Find Near Me")

The home page has a "Find Near Me" button. If you press it, your browser asks whether to share your location. If you allow it, your location is used only inside your browser to sort coffee shops by distance. It is not sent to our servers or stored by us. You can deny or withdraw location permission at any time in your browser settings.

Submitting or updating a coffee shop

The submit page lets anyone add a coffee shop or suggest changes to an existing listing. When you send the form, the details you enter are sent to our server at api.localcoffeeshop.co:

  • the shop's name, street address, city, state and ZIP code;
  • the shop's phone number and website, if you provide them;
  • a price level and a description, if you provide them.

These are details about a business, intended for the public directory. The form does not ask for your own name or email address, so please don't include personal information about yourself or anyone else in the description.
