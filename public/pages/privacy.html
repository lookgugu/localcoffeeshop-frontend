Privacy Policy

What this site collects and the choices you have.

Last updated: October 20, 2026

The short version

  • You don't need an account, and we never ask for your name or email to browse.
  • If you join the roaster-subscription interest list, we use your email address only for that, and only after you confirm it.
  • We use Google Analytics to see how the site is used. The cookie banner lets you turn off analytics cookies and storage. If you do, Google's tag can still send a small number of cookieless pings (see below).
  • Your cookie choice stays in your own browser.
  • "Find Near Me" uses your location only inside your browser. It is never sent to us.
  • If you submit a coffee shop, we receive the shop details you type in, and nothing else about you.

Analytics and cookies

Most pages load Google Analytics. When you allow analytics cookies, Google may set cookies such as _ga to tell visits apart. You can choose Accept All, Reject All or Customize in the cookie banner, and we pass your choice to Google using Google Consent Mode. We don't show ads, and advertising cookies stay off unless you allow them.

Before you choose, analytics cookies and storage are denied by default for visitors who appear to be in Europe, the UK or Switzerland, in a California or UTC time zone (based on your browser's time zone and language, checked in your browser), and allowed by default elsewhere. Advertising cookies are denied by default everywhere.

When analytics cookies and storage are denied (by default or because you chose Reject All), Google Analytics sets no analytics cookie and stores nothing in your browser, but the Google tag can still send limited, cookieless pings to Google: for example that a page was viewed or a sign-up was attempted. They contain no cookie identifier, and never your email address, the confirmation link or your search text. We strip those from the page address and from the referrer before the tag is configured. If the tag can't be configured that way, it isn't configured at all.

Your choice is saved in your browser's local storage as cookie_consent. To change it, clear this site's data in your browser and the banner will appear again. See how Google uses data from sites that use its services.

Other things stored on your device

  • Offline copies of the site. A service worker saves the site's own pages, scripts and styles so the site loads faster. These copies contain nothing about you.
  • A Cloudflare security cookie (__cf_bm, about 30 minutes) that helps block bots.

Submitting a coffee shop

The submit page sends the shop's name, address, phone, website, price level and description to our server. These are details about a business for the public directory, so please don't include personal information in them.

Roaster-subscription interest list

The homepage and the roasters page have a form for people interested in a possible monthly coffee subscription featuring local roasters. It is an interest list only: there is no payment or order, and the price, roasters and timing are not decided.

  • What we collect. The email address you type in. Ticking the consent box is required.
  • Confirmation first. We send one email with a confirmation link. You are added to the list only if you open that link and press "Confirm my interest". If you don't, nothing is added.
  • Who handles it. Our email provider, Resend, sends the email and stores the list of confirmed addresses. We don't keep a separate copy, and we don't put your address in our server logs.
  • What we send. Only emails about this subscription idea.
  • Unsubscribe any time. Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. You can also contact us to be removed. If you previously unsubscribed from all our emails, confirming won't sign you back up.
  • Analytics. Your email address, the confirmation link and anything you type into search boxes are never sent to Google Analytics. We only count anonymous events, such as "a sign-up was attempted", "a sign-up request was accepted" (this does not mean an email was sent) or "a sign-up was confirmed".

Services we use

Like every website, our servers and these providers receive your IP address and browser information when your browser connects to them. Each handles that information under its own privacy policy.

  • DigitalOcean hosts the site and its data.
  • Cloudflare delivers pages and protects against abuse.
  • Google provides Google Analytics and Google Fonts.
  • Resend sends and stores emails for the roaster-subscription interest list (only if you sign up).

Questions or requests

To ask about this policy or about information related to you, please visit our contact page. If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too.
